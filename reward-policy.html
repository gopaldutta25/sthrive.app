Reward & Earnings Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Important Disclaimer: Sthrive.app is a community engagement application. It is NOT an investment scheme, financial product, job platform, or get-rich-quick service. Rewards are promotional incentives granted for valid engagement.

1. How Sthrive Coins Work

Sthrive Coins are virtual points earned inside the platform by completing verified daily challenges, skill quizzes, and promotional tasks. Coins have no cash value outside of the Sthrive.app redemption ecosystem.

2. Redemption Eligibility & Thresholds

Users may request redemption once they reach the minimum threshold displayed in their app wallet. All redemptions are subject to anti-fraud review before completion.

3. Task Verification & Fraud Prevention

To ensure fair distribution of rewards, all tasks undergo verification checks:

  • Incomplete or skipped tasks will not award points.
  • Automated scripts, bots, VPNs, or fake referrals result in instant coin forfeiture.
  • Multiple accounts linked to a single individual or device will be disqualified.

4. Availability of Reward Stock

Gift cards and digital reward options are subject to availability and regional restrictions. Sthrive.app reserves the right to modify or substitute reward methods when necessary.

5. Modification of Reward Rates

Sthrive.app reserves the right to adjust coin values, daily reward task limits, and minimum withdrawal thresholds at any time to preserve platform integrity.

6. Questions Regarding Rewards

If you have questions regarding pending coin verification or redemptions, please reach out to sthrive.support@gmail.com.
