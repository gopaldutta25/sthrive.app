Account Management

Delete Your Sthrive Account

If you want to delete your account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Email us at sthrive.support@gmail.com from the email address registered with your Sthrive account.
  2. Include your username and an explicit request to delete your account.

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Profile information (name, email, phone number if provided)
  • Reward, earnings, and referral data
  • Task and quiz completion history
Data Retention Note: Some anonymized analytics data may be retained for up to 90 days for internal reporting and fraud prevention purposes.

Once processed, your account will be permanently removed from Sthrive.
