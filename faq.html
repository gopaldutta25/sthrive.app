Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about Sthrive.app

What is Sthrive.app?

Sthrive.app is a community engagement platform where users participate in local group activities, complete surveys, take daily skill quizzes, and earn reward points for active participation.

How do I earn Sthrive Coins?

You earn coins by engaging in daily challenges, maintaining streak milestones, completing community feedback surveys, and inviting verified friends to join your group.

How do I redeem my rewards?

Once you reach the minimum balance required (e.g., 500 coins), you can submit a redemption request in the wallet tab for direct digital transfers or gift vouchers.

How long does payout processing take?

Most redemption requests are processed within 24 to 48 hours after security verification.

Why were my coins deducted or pending?

Coins remain pending while task completion is verified. Deductions or cancellations occur if automated system checks flag fake task completion, duplicate accounts, or VPN usage.

How can I contact support?

You can email our support team anytime at sthrive.support@gmail.com or use our Contact Page.
